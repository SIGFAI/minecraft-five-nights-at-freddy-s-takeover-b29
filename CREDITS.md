# Credits

All art, models, textures and sounds in this mod are original or generated for it:

- Item icons (pizza slice, flashlight, bear plush) and the jumpscare picture: generated with fal (Nano Banana 2), then cut out and pixelated with the kit tools.
- Sound effects (jumpscare scream, music box, robot hit, robot death, robot step, power cut, 6 AM bell, honk): generated with fal (ElevenLabs sound effects).
- Robot skins (Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, Foxy) and their glowing eyes: drawn pixel by pixel by `tools/skins.py`.
- Robot models, pizzeria build, code: original, written for this mod.

No assets from any commercial game were used. The characters are original robot mascots inspired by the genre.

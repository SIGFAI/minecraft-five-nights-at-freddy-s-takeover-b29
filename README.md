# Five Nights at Freddy's Takeover

**Minecraft** × Five Nights at Freddy's · visual · idea by the team

Bring Five Nights at Freddy's into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned Minecraft into a haunted pizzeria: robot mascots wake at 12 AM, and Foxy only freezes if you stare at him. Survive until 6 AM!

## Install

Install Minecraft Java 26.3 with Fabric Loader and Fabric API, drop the mod jar in your mods folder, and start a survival world. A pizzeria appears near spawn, zombies turn into robots, and the flashlight, plush and pizza are in the creative Tools tab.
